## ACT 1: The Business Problem — The Cost of Screen Switching (00:00 – 01:30)

### Visual on Screen (Chrome Tab 1)
- Start on **Chrome Tab 1** (Standard Salesforce Lightning Home Page **without** the `[✨ Ask]` button).
- Click the standard Global Search bar, type `Dallas`, and hit Enter to show the standard list of static search results.

### Spoken Script
> *"Hello everyone, and thank you for joining us today.*
>
> *Before we talk about AI, let’s talk about a daily reality inside almost every enterprise we work with across the US and UK.*
>
> *Right now, you’re looking at a standard Salesforce environment—the exact screen your sales leaders, account executives, and service managers use every single day. If an executive wants an answer before a client call—like 'Which of our key Dallas accounts need attention today?'—look at what happens when we search.*
>
> *We get a list of disconnected records. Now your team has to open 10 to 15 different browser tabs—Accounts, Cases, Chatter notes, emails, and files—just to piece together one clear answer. In high-wage enterprise teams, employees lose **25% to 35% of their workday** to this constant screen switching and manual searching.*
>
> *That isn't a technology gap—that is a massive productivity tax."*

## ACT 2: The Shift & How Agentforce Coworker Actually Works (01:30 – 03:00)

### Mouse Action (Switch to Chrome Tab 2)
- Click directly over to **Chrome Tab 2** (same Lightning interface, now with **`[✨ Ask]`** right beside Global Search).
- Slowly hover over **`[✨ Ask]`** and click it to open the conversational workspace.

### Spoken Script (Explaining How It Works Without Setup Jargon)
> *"Now look at **this** tab. Same Salesforce workspace your teams already know—zero new software to install, zero retraining required. The only difference is one button right next to Search: **`[✨ Ask]`**.*
>
> *So, how does Agentforce Coworker actually work behind the scenes for your business?*
>
> *Think of it in three simple steps—not as a generic chatbot, but as a trusted digital teammate:*
>
> *1. **It Understands Business Language:** Your team doesn't need rigid search terms or complex reports. They ask questions the same way they’d ask a senior colleague.*
> *2. **It Connects Your Full Business Memory:** Behind the scenes, Coworker bridges structured CRM data (accounts, pipeline, service cases) with unstructured context—like call notes, emails, and knowledge documents—synthesizing one clear brief.*
> *3. **It Operates Strictly Inside Your Rules:** Unlike public AI tools, Coworker never leaks data or breaks governance. It automatically inherits each employee's existing Salesforce permissions, so a rep only ever sees what they are already authorized to view."*

## ACT 3: End-User Features & Business Benefits in Action (03:00 – 06:00)

### Feature 1: "FIND" — Executive Decision Speed with Proof (03:00 – 04:00)

#### Copy-Paste Prompt
```text
What are our top merchant accounts in Dallas, Texas and what is their current status?
```

#### Business Value to Highlight
- Hover over the **clickable citation pills / record links** inside the synthesized answer.

#### Spoken Script
> *"Let’s see what this looks like for an account leader preparing for a customer review.*
>
> *I ask in plain English: 'What are our top merchant accounts in Dallas, Texas and what is their current status?'*
>
> *In seconds—instead of opening 12 browser tabs—Coworker reads across our accounts and open activities and gives us a clean executive brief. More importantly for leadership: look at these **clickable source citations**. There is no guesswork and no AI black box. One click verifies the exact source record."*

---

### Feature 2: "CATCH UP" — Instant Leadership & Escalation Visibility (04:00 – 05:00)

#### Copy-Paste Prompt
```text
Catch me up on high-priority cases and escalations logged this week.
```

#### Business Value to Highlight
- Point out how Coworker filters urgent blockers from routine noise across records and case notes.

#### Spoken Script
> *"Second end-user capability: **Instant Catch-Up**.*
>
> *Think about a VP of Customer Success returning from travel, or a regional director walking into a Monday morning review. Normally, catching up takes an hour of chasing status updates across Slack, Teams, and email.*
>
> *With one prompt, Coworker scans high-priority cases and escalations logged this week, highlights what actually threatens customer retention, and surfaces where leadership needs to step in today."*

---

### Feature 3: "PLAN & ACT" — Faster Execution with Human Approval (05:00 – 06:00)

#### Copy-Paste Prompt
```text
Draft a follow-up email to the account owner of our top escalated merchant proposing a resolution and a 10% promotional credit.
```

#### Business Value to Highlight
- Show the **Interactive Draft / Confirmation Preview**—highlighting that nothing commits or sends without human sign-off.
- Mention guided record updates (such as updating Contact details or case follow-ups directly from chat).

#### Spoken Script
> *"Third capability: **Moving Work Forward Safely**.*
>
> *Insights only create ROI when they turn into action. Here, Coworker not only summarized the top escalated merchant—it drafted a context-aware outreach proposal tailored to that exact issue.*
>
> *And here is why C-level leaders trust this design: **Human-in-the-Loop control**. Whether Coworker is drafting an executive follow-up or updating a customer contact record through your approved business workflows, it always stops to ask the employee for confirmation first. Speed without losing operational control."*
