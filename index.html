ACT 1 The Business Problem: Cost of Screen Switching (00:00 – 01:30)

🖥️ Chrome Tab 1 (Without Ask Button): Start on the standard Lightning Home Page. Type Dallas into standard Global Search and hit Enter to display static record lists.
"Hello everyone, and thank you for joining us today.

Before we talk about AI, let’s talk about a daily reality inside almost every enterprise we work with across the US and UK.

Right now, you’re looking at a standard Salesforce environment—the exact screen your sales leaders, account executives, and service managers use every single day. If an executive wants an answer before a client call—like 'Which of our key Dallas accounts need attention today?'—look at what happens when we search.

We get a list of disconnected records. Now your team has to open 10 to 15 different browser tabs—Accounts, Cases, Chatter notes, emails, and files—just to piece together one clear answer. In high-wage enterprise teams, employees lose 25% to 35% of their workday to this constant screen switching and manual searching.

That isn't a technology gap—that is a massive productivity tax."

ACT 2 How Agentforce Coworker Actually Works (01:30 – 03:00)

🖥️ Chrome Tab 2 (With [✨ Ask] Button): Switch directly to Tab 2. Hover over the [✨ Ask] button beside Global Search and click it to launch the conversational workspace.
"Now look at this tab. Same Salesforce workspace your teams already know—zero new software to install, zero retraining required. The only difference is one button right next to Search: [✨ Ask].

So, how does Agentforce Coworker actually work behind the scenes for your business? Think of it in three simple steps—not as a generic chatbot, but as a trusted digital teammate:

1. It Understands Business Language: Your team doesn't need rigid search terms or complex reports. They ask questions the same way they’d ask a senior colleague.

2. It Connects Your Full Business Memory: Behind the scenes, Coworker bridges structured CRM data (accounts, pipeline, service cases) with unstructured context—like call notes, emails, and knowledge documents—synthesizing one clear brief.

3. It Operates Strictly Inside Your Rules: Unlike public AI tools, Coworker never leaks data or breaks governance. It automatically inherits each employee's existing Salesforce permissions, so a rep only ever sees what they are already authorized to view."
